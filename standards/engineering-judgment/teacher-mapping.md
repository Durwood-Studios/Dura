# Teacher mapping and source review

Reviewed September 2026. These are **instructional connections**, not an official crosswalk, accreditation finding, product conformance audit or professional certification. Every source is publicly linked in the case catalog.

| Source and verified scope                                                                                                                                                               | Dura evidence to discuss                                                                                                                              | Scope boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [ABET Engineering Criteria 2026–2027, Criterion 3 outcomes 2, 4, 6](https://www.abet.org/accreditation/accreditation-criteria/criteria-for-accrediting-engineering-programs-2026-2027/) | Design constraints, stakeholder consequences, interpretation of experiments in release, telemetry, robotics and cost cases                            | Individual cases do not satisfy whole-program accreditation requirements. Communication is also relevant but is not claimed as a complete outcome-3 mapping.                                                     |
| [SWEBOK v4 knowledge areas](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/topics)                                                                         | Requirements, architecture, testing, configuration management, maintenance and economics in sync, release, performance and maintenance-cost decisions | Topic-level alignment, not a clause audit or IEEE certificate.                                                                                                                                                   |
| [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)                                                                                                                                               | Reflow 1.4.10, keyboard 2.1.1, focus visible 2.4.7, name/role/value 4.1.2 in the popup case                                                           | Expanded-state announcement concerns 4.1.2; a popup opening is not automatically a 4.1.3 status message. Test focus not obscured 2.4.11 where applicable. A 320-CSS-pixel test does not prove all WCAG criteria. |
| [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)                                                                                                                | Govern, Map, Measure and Manage guide context, representative evaluation, ownership and response in the tutor case                                    | Voluntary risk framework; no universal permissible error rate or NIST product approval.                                                                                                                          |
| [ACM Code of Ethics](https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics)                                                                                                                                | Harm, privacy, honest evaluation and limits of competence in sync, AI and robotics decisions                                                          | Ethical principles support reasoning but do not specify machinery safety limits or authorize operation.                                                                                                          |
| [IES/WWC practice guide 1](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/practiceguide/1)                                                                                                                 | Explanation, worked comparison, delayed retrieval and transfer discussion                                                                             | Recommendation evidence is not validation of Dura, the six-dimension rubric or professional competency. See EJ-1.0 for exact evidence ratings.                                                                   |

These twelve discussion entry points are not twelve complete profession-specific assessments. Hardware verification currently uses a transfer prompt, not a dedicated verification-signoff case. Teachers must teach the new-domain facts before evaluating a transfer response; absence of such facts cannot fairly be scored as weak judgment.
