# Career study coverage

The September 2026 review found 106 distinct career-topic identifiers without study entry points. All 106 now resolve to specific introductory teaching. The current inventory is 749 phase lessons, 120 modules and 1,806 assessment questions. These counts describe authored coverage; they do not establish professional proficiency, an entire role level's depth or independent qualification.

The historical missing-topic set comprises 14 required and 28 valuable-only topics on published path destinations, plus 64 topics used only by adjacent careers. Paths explicitly distinguish destination careers from related careers. Systems, hardware verification and quantitative engineering are not silently equated with an unrelated career record.

The committed source is the detailed, reproducible mapping:

- [Role requirements](../../src/content/roles.ts), [canonical skill lessons](../../src/content/skill-lessons.ts), and [additional study topics](../../src/content/role-study-topics.ts).
- [Path destinations and related careers](../../src/lib/paths/index.ts) and [coverage resolution](../../src/lib/career-coverage.ts).
- [Destination-topic regression](../../tests/curriculum/required-career-bridges.test.ts), [semantic destination regression](../../tests/curriculum/semantic-career-bridges.test.ts), and [adjacent-topic regression](../../tests/curriculum/adjacent-career-bridges.test.ts).

The final adjacent-career expansion supplies 48 lessons and 56 questions, including a 12-lesson financial transaction systems module. Exercises use synthetic ledgers, payment states, webhook reconciliation, reporting controls, currency legs, liquidity timing and consent. No money movement, regulatory filing or bank integration was performed. Safety-standard introductions explain scope and evidence obligations; they neither replace the licensed standards nor approve machinery, medical devices, vehicles or aircraft for use.

Local executable evidence includes Python/SQLite CSV and ETL contracts, ledger/reconciliation controls, consent predicates, streaming windows, CRDT merges, finite reinforcement-learning returns, causal attention invariance and a Node deep-link parser. A synthetic mobile markup fixture passed a 320-pixel browser check with keyboard activation and action geometry. That fixture does not establish native mobile behavior.

All authored lesson, tutorial and how-to MDX is evaluated in a render regression with interactive leaf components substituted. Full production browser checks separately exercise rendered page geometry and critical learning workflows. Neither check proves every technical claim or executes every embedded program.

Airflow and Spark examples were syntax-reviewed without those engines. React Native/Expo code was not built on Android or iOS. Push providers, app stores, service meshes, Kubernetes, GitOps, edge/CDN, bank/security profiles and data-lake integrations were not deployed. ROS/URsim, Zephyr, proprietary qualified toolchains and Apex execution also remain external acceptance work. Actual platform execution, specialist review, advanced project experience and empirical Dura teaching effectiveness remain separate requirements.
