# Specialty module subject mapping review

Reviewed 2026-09-12 against the authored lesson titles, objectives, mechanisms and exercises in phases 10–14. These are educational subject references, **not** certification, professional accreditation, full-standard conformance, or evidence that a learner can commission a safe machine. Pedagogy and technical review of individual lessons remains a separate requirement.

The previous map described hardware verification modules as PCB design, JTAG and EMC, quantitative C++ modules as financial mathematics, and several manufacturing modules as unrelated topics. Those mappings and unsupported module-wide SFIA role/level assertions have been removed. Per-lesson competency metadata remains separate. All 52 specialty modules have a decision below; an empty module claim is intentional, not a missing mapping.

A referenced edition is the edition taught, not a claim that it is the latest applicable edition. C++23 is published as **ISO/IEC 14882:2024**, not “14882:2023”; the affected lesson references are corrected. UPF material currently teaches the 2018 edition; the publisher now also lists 2024. No blanket edition migration or normative-clause certification is implied.
