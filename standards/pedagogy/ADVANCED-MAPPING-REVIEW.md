# Advanced module mapping review

Reviewed against the lesson inventory on 2026-09-11. These seven modules use
`lesson-metadata-only` deliberately: their subject matter does not establish a
whole-module K–12 or professional certification equivalence. Empty module claims
are preferable to manufacturing an AP, CSTA, SFIA work-role, or regulatory mapping.

| Module | Evidence in lesson scope                                                                            | Mapping decision                                                                                                                    |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 6-8    | Five lessons on linear algebra, regression, trees, SVMs and model evaluation                        | Retain individual mathematical/algorithmic learning metadata; no module-wide AP or professional credential claim.                   |
| 6-9    | Four lessons on image arrays, convolution, detection and production vision                          | Retain individual AI learning metadata; no blanket IEEE ethics or K–12 equivalence inferred from the use of AI.                     |
| 7-5    | Five lessons combine formal models, bounded checking, GPU architecture and CUDA                     | Mixed scope does not establish one work role or safety certification. Model checking is explicitly limited by the model and bounds. |
| 8-6    | Five system-design lessons cover requirements, scaling, URL shortening, notifications and tradeoffs | Design exercises are educational evidence, not proof of a professional responsibility level.                                        |
| 8-7    | Four lessons cover service objectives, on-call work, incident response and postmortems              | Operational incidents do not automatically constitute the cybersecurity work roles in NICE. No inferred NICE role.                  |
| 9-9    | Four lessons cover technical diligence, architecture debt, team assessment and integration          | No acquisition, audit or management qualification inferred from case-study completion.                                              |
| 9-10   | Four lessons discuss compliance scope, SOC 2, privacy engineering and security programs             | Subject coverage does not establish compliance, auditor qualification, or legal sufficiency. No blanket certification mapping.      |

This is a review of module-level claims and their limits. Existing individual
lesson metadata is not independently certified by this document. A future mapping
must name the framework edition and connect its actual criteria to assessed work.
