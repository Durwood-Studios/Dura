## Why this matters

A new retrieval method scores better on one run, so a team calls it a breakthrough. The improvement could instead come from a changed dataset, extra compute or choosing the best of many attempts. A credible research comparison makes those alternatives inspectable.

## Start with something familiar

To compare two recipes, use the same ingredients and serving conditions. If one receives twice the cooking time, the experiment tests more than the recipe change. Recording the plan before tasting helps prevent redefining success around the preferred result.

## The mental model

> A research claim needs a testable question, a controlled comparison, reproducible records and stated limits. A favorable result supports the tested claim under its conditions; it does not establish causation or generality beyond the design.

## The mechanism

Start with a narrow question: does reranker B improve correct top-one retrieval over A on the same held-out query set under a declared latency budget? Record dataset version, eligibility and exclusions, training/development/test separation, primary metric, compute conditions and the comparison procedure before viewing final results. Include a relevant baseline rather than only the previous experimental variant.

An ablation removes or changes one component to probe its contribution. It is useful only when other material conditions remain comparable. For stochastic systems, retain seeds and repeated runs; repeated runs on one dataset do not create independent evidence about all future populations.

In a synthetic100-query paired result, both methods succeed on 60 queries, only A succeeds on 10, only B on 20, and neither on 10. A accuracy is 70%, B80%, a10 percentage-point observed difference. The30 disagreements are especially relevant to a paired comparison. This table alone does not provide a confidence interval or prove the change will generalize. A separate future evaluation is needed if the team selected B after repeatedly inspecting this test set.

A result record should preserve the code/configuration, data version or reproducible fixture, all attempted variants, failures and resource use. Document limitations: sample selection, leakage risks, metric mismatch and deployment constraints. Distinguish reproducing a result under the original setup from testing it independently in a changed setting. A paper’s prestige cannot substitute for inspecting whether its evidence answers your question.

For Dura practice, the supplied table is intentionally small and artificial. The exercise is to produce an auditable comparison plan, not to claim a statistically significant discovery. Do not invent a p-value or confidence interval without a specified model and calculation.
