## Why this matters

A transfer is marked complete when its first currency leg settles, even though the beneficiary leg is still pending. A cross-border workflow needs evidence for each obligation and the conversion between them.

## Start with something familiar

Exchanging two different tickets requires checking both handovers. A receipt for the first ticket is not proof that the second was delivered.

## The mental model

> Track currency, amount, counterparty, timing and settlement state for each leg. A foreign-exchange quote, a payment instruction and a settled beneficiary credit are different evidence.

## The mechanism

This synthetic example sends 100 USD at an agreed training rate of 0.90 EUR per USD, with a separately disclosed 2 EUR beneficiary-side fee. The converted amount is 90 EUR and the net expected credit is 88 EUR. These invented terms are not market prices, provider recommendations or a universal fee model.

```python
from decimal import Decimal
source = Decimal("100.00")
rate = Decimal("0.90")
fee = Decimal("2.00")
converted = source * rate
net = converted - fee
assert converted == Decimal("90.0000")
assert net == Decimal("88.0000")
assert net + fee == converted
print("currency-leg reconciliation passed")
```

Never add 100 USD and 90 EUR as if they were one currency balance. A real ledger uses explicit currency accounts and conversion postings under its accounting policy. Define decimal scale and rounding rules, quote identity/expiry, fee allocation and the reconciliation source. This arithmetic fixture does not implement such a ledger or an FX service.

Trace states independently: source funds reserved, source leg settled, conversion confirmed, destination leg submitted and destination credit confirmed under the actual scheme. If the source leg settles while the destination fails, retain an unresolved obligation and investigate its recovery or return process. Do not manufacture success or initiate repeated payments without idempotent operation identities.

Payment-versus-payment arrangements can link settlement of two currencies to reduce principal settlement risk under their specific rules. They do not mean every retail transfer is simultaneous or that all liquidity, operational and legal risks disappear. Operating hours, intermediaries and local settlement arrangements affect when each leg can complete.

For the exercise, the quote is accepted at 10:00, the source settles at 10:01 and the destination remains pending at 10:03. The product may report source settled / destination pending; it has no evidence for beneficiary credited. If a later provider report shows 87 EUR credited, reconcile the one-euro difference against the agreed fee and conversion terms instead of silently changing the expected value.
