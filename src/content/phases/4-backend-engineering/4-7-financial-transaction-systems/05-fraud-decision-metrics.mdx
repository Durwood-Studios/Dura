## Why this matters

A fraud rule catches most labeled fraud but blocks many legitimate customers. A single accuracy or recall number cannot decide whether the policy is useful, fair or operationally manageable.

## Start with something familiar

A smoke alarm that sounds all day may detect every fire but also make the building unusable. Detection needs both missed-event and false-alarm evidence, tied to the consequences of each.

## The mental model

> Fraud detection is a decision under uncertainty. Evaluate false positives, false negatives, review capacity and delayed labels separately from the model score; a flagged transaction is not proof of wrongdoing.

## The mechanism

The synthetic evaluation set contains10000 transactions:100 later labeled fraud and9900 legitimate. A rule flags80 fraud and198 legitimate transactions. It misses20 fraud. Precision is 80/278, about28.8%; recall is 80/100=80%; false-positive rate is 198/9900=2%. The class imbalance makes the operational burden visible.

A threshold should be chosen on development data with explicit costs and constraints, then evaluated on untouched data. Possible actions include allow, request more verification or route to review; they need not all be irreversible blocks. Human review has finite capacity, and delayed labels can make recent performance look artificially good or bad.

Keep outcome labels, event times, feature availability and model versions. A feature learned only after chargeback cannot be used as if it were known at authorization time. Split evaluation to reflect temporal deployment and inspect relevant subgroups and channels for uneven error rates. Aggregate accuracy alone can conceal harmful patterns.

The fixture does not establish the monetary cost of an error, a legally appropriate policy or a production fraud model. Those require the product, jurisdiction, customer-impact and operational context. Avoid presenting a score as an accusation; preserve an accountable review and correction path where appropriate.

Monitor drift, alert volume and downstream outcomes after deployment, with controlled changes and auditability. Do not optimize the model only for catching more labeled fraud if the result overwhelms reviewers or prevents legitimate use.
