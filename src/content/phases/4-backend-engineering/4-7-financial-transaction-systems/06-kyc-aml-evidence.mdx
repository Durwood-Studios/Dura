## Why this matters

A system labels a customer verified because an image-upload step completed. Collecting a document, verifying identity, assessing risk and making an onboarding decision are separate processes with different evidence.

## Start with something familiar

A completed application form is not the same as an approved application. Each review step needs its own criteria, responsible party and record of what was actually established.

## The mental model

> A customer due-diligence pipeline should preserve evidence, policy version and accountable decisions. International standards inform national regimes; they do not create one universal checklist or automated approval rule.

## The mechanism

FATF Recommendation10 addresses customer due diligence, including identification/verification, beneficial ownership where relevant, purpose/nature of the relationship and ongoing due diligence. Actual obligations, thresholds, accepted evidence and reporting procedures depend on applicable law, regulator and institutional policy. Verify that context with qualified compliance ownership before implementation.

The fictional workflow uses submitted, evidence-incomplete, review-required, approved and rejected states. A document upload only moves to submitted. A mismatch between declared name and verification evidence moves to review-required under the supplied policy; it is not automatically fraud or a reason to publish an accusation.

Record the policy version, evidence references, verification result, risk factors considered, reviewer identity, decision time and review schedule. Minimize sensitive copies and restrict access. Do not log identity documents or secrets in generic application logs. Preserve correction and appeal/review processes according to the institution’s obligations.

Screening matches require careful handling of false positives and the governing regime. Ongoing monitoring is not satisfied by a one-time onboarding flag. Changes in customer information or relevant risk can require renewed review under policy. Automated tools support decisions but do not remove accountable governance.

This teaching fixture supplies a process model, not legal advice, a regulatory filing or a production identity-verification service. It intentionally does not invent universal monetary thresholds, retention periods or mandatory outcomes. Those must be mapped to a specific current jurisdiction and product before a real pipeline is approved.
