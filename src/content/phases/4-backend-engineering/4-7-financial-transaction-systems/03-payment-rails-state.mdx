## Why this matters

An API returns accepted, and an application immediately treats the money as irrevocably settled. Different payment systems have different processing, settlement and exception rules; one generic paid flag hides those distinctions.

## Start with something familiar

A purchase order, a dispatched shipment and a completed delivery are related but different milestones. Payment instructions likewise pass through states whose financial meaning depends on the rail.

## The mental model

> Model the chosen rail’s documented states and finality rules. Submission or acceptance is not a universal settlement guarantee, and reversal/return behavior must be designed explicitly.

## The mechanism

A payment rail is infrastructure and rules for moving payment instructions and settling obligations. For example, FedACH supports batched debit/credit processing, while Fedwire Funds provides real-time settlement with finality under its governing rules. These examples illustrate different models; actual eligibility, cutoffs, operating calendars and legal rules must be checked for the chosen service.

The synthetic application uses submitted, accepted, settled and returned states. A submitted instruction has a client reference. Accepted means the provider received it under the fixture contract; settled requires the rail’s settlement evidence. A later return is a new event with its own reason and accounting effect, not deletion of the original history. Not every rail permits every transition in this simplified model.

Suppose instructionP1 for1000 minor units is accepted at 09:00 and a settlement record appears at 11:00. Before11:00 the application can report accepted/pending settlement, not settled. If an allowed return event later arrives, record it and reconcile the corresponding ledger correction according to the rail and product contract.

Track amount, currency, parties, stable references, timestamps, status evidence and exceptions. Reconcile provider statements or settlement reports to the ledger. Handle business days, cutoff times and timezone definitions without assuming every day is a processing day. Do not promise a transfer time based only on an API response.

This is a rail-state literacy exercise, not an instruction to initiate a transfer or a universal legal-finality model. A real integration needs the exact participant agreement and operational rules, reviewed accounting treatment and an executed test environment.
