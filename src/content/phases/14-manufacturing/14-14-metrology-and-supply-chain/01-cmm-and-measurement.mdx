## Why this matters

A dimension displayed to three decimal places is not known exactly to three decimal places. Near a specification limit, measurement uncertainty changes what a result supports. A defensible inspection record separates the measured estimate, its uncertainty, and the agreed decision rule.

## Start with something familiar

A location pin on a map may come with an accuracy circle. Being close to a boundary does not tell you which side the actual location occupies. Measurement estimates have a similar need for a stated uncertainty, although the uncertainty model and coverage must be defined rather than imagined as an absolute bound.

## The mental model

> A CMM result depends on the characteristic, alignment, sampling, corrections, and uncertainty model. Conformance is a decision made from that result under a specified rule.

## Define the characteristic and measurement plan

A coordinate measuring machine (CMM) gathers coordinate observations used to evaluate a specified feature or characteristic. A datum supplies a reference for interpreting a requirement. The measurement plan identifies the part revision, feature, datum alignment, probe configuration, sampling strategy, and evaluation method. Three points may mathematically define an ideal plane, but sparse sampling does not establish that a real surface's form has been adequately assessed.

Probe qualification, calibration information, environmental effects, workpiece behavior, repeatability, and alignment can contribute to uncertainty. Display resolution alone is not the uncertainty. Repeated readings characterize some effects but do not automatically remove systematic effects or all sampling bias.

## A bounded uncertainty budget

Standard uncertainty is expressed as a standard-deviation-like quantity. For independent contributions with sensitivity coefficients of 1, combine by root-sum-square. In this teaching model the contributions are 3 micrometres and 4 micrometres, so combined standard uncertainty is square-root of (9+16)=5 micrometres. With stipulated coverage factor k=2, expanded uncertainty U=10 micrometres=0.010 mm.

Independence and the sensitivity coefficients are assumptions, not universal defaults. Correlated inputs require covariance terms; a different measurement function changes the coefficients. Nor does k=2 universally mean exactly95 percent coverage. A report states the model, factor, and supported interpretation.

## Apply the declared interval rule

For this exercise only, accept when the entire interval from y-U to y+U lies within specification limits L and H. Declare demonstrated nonconformance when the interval lies entirely outside the specification. An overlapping interval remains unresolved under this rule. This three-way teaching rule is explicitly supplied; contractual decision rules can differ.

With limits 24.980 to25.020 mm and y=25.005 mm, U=0.010 mm gives interval24.995 to25.015 mm, wholly inside. With y=25.015 mm, interval25.005 to25.025 overlaps the upper limit: it is not demonstrated conforming under this rule, but the overlap does not demonstrate that the true value is outside.

Use the actual lower and upper limits. Collapsing an asymmetric tolerance to the smaller absolute deviation silently changes the specification. An empty measurement record also cannot establish an inspected characteristic passed.
