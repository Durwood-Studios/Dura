## Why this matters

Two dashboards report different OEE for the same shift because one excludes a planned stop and the other does not. Multiplying three percentages correctly does not make the denominators comparable.

## Start with something familiar

Two runners cannot compare race times fairly if one timer excludes a rest break. A shared number needs shared start, stop and inclusion rules.

## The mental model

> A KPI is a definition plus its data and scope, not only a formula. Compare equipment performance only after time, count, unit and reporting boundaries agree.

## The mechanism

ISO22400 defines a framework for manufacturing operations KPIs; Part2 describes selected indicators through formulas, elements, time behavior, units and other characteristics. Its public scope points to work-unit context. The detailed applicable edition and project definitions must be consulted before claiming a standard-specific implementation. This lesson uses an explicitly declared teaching OEE formula, not a substitute for the licensed normative definitions.

For the fixture, availability equals runtime/planned production time; performance equals ideal cycle time × total count/runtime; quality equals good count/total count. All times use minutes and both counts concern the same interval and unit. With planned400, runtime320, ideal cycle0.5, total600 and good570, the factors are0.8,0.9375 and0.95. Their product is0.7125, or71.25%. The equivalent check is ideal good time/planned time =285/400.

If another report removes40 minutes from planned time while retaining the same other values, the resulting285/360 is79.17% rounded. That increase does not show more good parts or less actual runtime loss; the denominator changed. Record the reason and keep comparison periods consistent.

A KPI definition card should identify formula/version, work unit, interval, time inclusion rules, ideal-cycle basis, count definitions, data sources, missing-data handling and owner. A performance factor above100% is a signal to review ideal-cycle and count/time consistency rather than silently cap the number to make a dashboard look plausible.

Investment decisions also need cost, feasibility, safety, demand and expected benefit. The factor whose hypothetical improvement moves OEE most is not automatically the best investment. A KPI highlights questions; it does not supply the whole business or engineering decision.

## Practice

A report rises from71.25% to79.17% with exactly the same570 good units because its planned-time denominator changed. What may be concluded?
