## Why this matters

A serial decoder displays random bytes, so the team changes firmware. The actual capture used the wrong logic threshold and sampled too slowly. Measurement configuration can create apparent protocol bugs.

## Start with something familiar

A camera taking one picture per second cannot reliably show a fast blinking light’s pattern. A digital capture also samples at discrete times and classifies voltage relative to a threshold.

## The mental model

> A decoder interprets sampled edges under a protocol configuration. Verify the electrical and time-resolution contract before treating decoded labels as observations of the transmitted bytes.

## The mechanism

A logic analyzer records digital channel states over time. Its threshold determines how input voltage is classified; its sample rate determines the spacing between observations. The appropriate input limits and threshold settings depend on the specific instrument model, so inspect its documentation and the signal specification before connection. This lesson uses synthetic captures and does not authorize probing energized industrial equipment.

For a supplied8N1 UART fixture at100000baud, one bit lasts10microseconds. A sample rate of2MHz has0.5microsecond spacing, giving20samples per nominal bit. This arithmetic describes resolution; it does not prove electrical compatibility or a universal minimum sample rate. Decoder settings must also match baud, polarity, data bits, parity and stop bits.

A capture record should retain channels, reference connection, instrument/model, permitted input range, threshold, sample rate, trigger, duration and protocol settings. Inspect raw edges around a known pattern such as0x55 before trusting a decoded stream. If a pulse appears only one sample wide, its width is poorly resolved; increasing the sample rate or investigating the signal may change the interpretation.

A digital trace does not show every analog quality issue. Ringing or marginal voltage may require an appropriate analog instrument and trained measurement setup. A decoder reporting the expected byte is not a proof of noise margin, setup/hold timing or system safety.

Compare two captures with the same firmware but different settings. If the raw data changes because the capture threshold was inappropriate, changing application logic is not a justified first repair. Retain the before/after configurations so the diagnosis can be reproduced.
