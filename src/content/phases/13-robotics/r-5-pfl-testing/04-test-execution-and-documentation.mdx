## Why this matters

A valid force trace attached to the wrong scenario is not useful validation evidence. Neither is a test whose fixture moved without a record. Execution controls connect the approved test plan to the measurements that actually occurred.

Think of each run as a laboratory sample with a chain of custody. Its identifier must connect the scenario, equipment, configuration, raw data, processing, and conclusion without relying on someone's memory.

> Preserve enough evidence to reconstruct each run, and hold results when that chain breaks. A passing measurement covers only the conditions for which the test method and coverage argument are justified.

## Prepare a controlled run

Record the application version, robot and tool identifiers, payload configuration, relevant safety settings, layout, scenario, and test-method revision. Identify the responsible personnel and the safe test procedure. Use instrumentation and controlled access; do not use a person as the contact target.

Confirm instrument checks required by the selected method and manufacturer, including configuration, range, acquisition, and fixture setup. A static check may detect an obvious force-channel problem, but it does not demonstrate adequate dynamic or pressure response. Record pre-test and post-test checks and investigate failures for their possible effect on prior results.

[A3's R15.806 overview](https://www.automate.org/robotics/news/ria-standards-committee-releases-new-technical-report-on-testing-power-and-force-in-collaborative-robots) describes a measurement-method report for quasi-static and transient contacts. Use its controlled requirements, or another applicable specified method, rather than treating this lesson as a substitute test standard.

## Match geometry and acquisition to the scenario

The fixture's position, orientation, restraint, and mechanical response must match the selected method. Record reference points and tolerances justified for the scenario. There is no universal ±5 mm tolerance in this lesson. A generic adult height does not establish coverage of actual work positions, and head-contact prevention cannot be replaced by a fixture at head height.

Choose the measurement window, trigger, range, sample rate, and filtering to preserve the relevant event. Verify the complete chain's response. A force value in newtons is not a contact velocity, and no fixed sampling rate follows from it alone. Check for clipping, saturation, missed triggers, incomplete windows, and unsuitable pressure media before interpreting a run.

Extract force and pressure using the selected method. **Peak force divided by nominal contact area is not a measurement of peak pressure.** Preserve raw records, calibration/configuration references, processing settings, and any correction or exclusion with its reason.

## Do not assume a convenient test is the worst case

A higher nominal payload or commanded speed does not by itself prove that every other configuration produces lower peak force and pressure. Pose-dependent effective mass, controller response, contact geometry, restraint, and tool compliance can change the result. A bounding argument must address these dependencies and the domain where it is valid.

Use the repeat count and coverage justified by the applicable method and plan; “three passes means validated” is not a universal rule. Configuration changes require an impact assessment identifying which results remain applicable and which must be repeated. Keep the old records linked to their original configuration instead of deleting inconvenient runs.

## Worked example: a reconstructable result

Consider a **fictional exercise** record: scenario S-07, configuration C-12, raw file R-041, measured force 47 N, expanded uncertainty 2 N. The supplied exercise rule is measured force + uncertainty ≤ 50 N. The numerical force comparison meets that rule because 49 N ≤ 50 N.

During review, the pressure channel is found saturated. The overall scenario cannot be accepted on the available evidence. Record the force comparison with its limited scope, hold the scenario result, correct the pressure measurement problem, and repeat the affected work under the documented plan. Do not replace the missing pressure evidence with a nominal-area calculation.

## Guided practice

Run R-042 uses configuration C-12, but a photograph shows the fixture at a different angle than the plan. The operator remembers that the angle was “probably better.” Write the immediate record disposition and the evidence needed next.

## What you learned

- Link every run to controlled configuration, geometry, method, and raw evidence.
- Hold results when fixture or acquisition problems undermine their applicability.
- Apply the declared numerical rule and preserve its limited scope.
- Justify test coverage and reassess affected evidence after changes.
